# taller

Runs my projects through an engineering team of Claude agents that I direct, on my own machine and on my subscription.

## Must never break

Writing a ticket's state to main. Everything Taller knows about a piece of work lives in git, and a wrong write loses the work.

One operator: the owner alone.

Reached from: this machine. Used on a phone: no.

## Data

Tickets with their plans, verdicts and notes inside each project's own git repository, and the hub's rules, profiles and brands.

Money, personal data or credentials: no.

## Deploys to

This machine only.
